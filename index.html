03 / NO MYSTERY MEAT

WHAT HAPPENS TO YOUR CODE AND KEYS?

  • ✓
    Your code stays in your GitHub.

    Deployinn does not keep a separate copy of your project code.

  • ✓
    Secret values stay out of chat.

    The setup assistant sees variable names, never your code or secret values.

  • ✓
    You stay in control.

    Manage projects and environment settings from the dashboard.

Heads up: repositories created by Deployinn and deployed sites are public. Never put secrets directly in your code.

06 / GOOD QUESTIONS

FAQ.
THE QUICK ANSWERS.

Which AI tools work with Deployinn?

Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and Grok in Chrome.

Do I need GitHub?

Yes. Deployinn saves your code to a repository in your own GitHub account.

What does it cost?

Free includes $5 of hosting usage a month and 3 projects. Pro is $29 per month. See pricing.

What happens when Free usage runs out?

Your apps pause until the 1st of the next month or until you upgrade. We email you at 80% usage.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. Free includes 1 and Pro includes 10 custom domains.

Is Deployinn made by Anthropic, OpenAI, Google or xAI?

No. Deployinn is an independent product.
