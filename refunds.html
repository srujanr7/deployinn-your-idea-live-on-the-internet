FAIR BILLING. NO GUESSWORK.

How subscription cancellation and refund requests work for Deployinn Pro.

Last updated: [PUBLICATION DATE]

Draft policy: confirm you can honour these refund windows before publishing. This is not legal advice.

Cancellation

Cancel Pro any time from Manage billing. You keep Pro until the end of the paid period, and it will not renew. Your account moves to Free; your apps are not deleted. Free limits apply, so apps exceeding included usage may pause until the next month. If you have more than 3 projects, you keep them but cannot create new ones until under the limit.

Refunds

  • First payment: Email within 7 days of your first payment for a full refund.
  • Renewals: If charged for a renewal you did not intend, email within 48 hours for a full refund.
  • Mistakes: Duplicate, incorrect or unauthorised charges are refunded in full at any time.
  • Otherwise: No partial refunds for unused time in a billing period.

How to request a refund

Email [SUPPORT_EMAIL] from the address on your account and include the charge date. We aim to reply within 2 business days. Refunds go to the original payment method and usually appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

Failed payments and chargebacks

Failed charges may be retried. Deploys may pause until you update your payment method; apps are not deleted. Please contact support before disputing a charge with your bank.

Who sells Pro?

Dodo Payments is the seller of record for Pro subscriptions and sends receipts and invoices.
