WE MAKE SHIPPING FEEL SIMPLE.

Deployinn closes the gap between getting AI to build something and getting it online.

Why Deployinn exists

AI tools can help people build a working app in a chat, but sharing it still often means learning hosting and deployment. Deployinn makes that next step more approachable with one button.

Who it's for

Designers, product managers, founders, students and curious builders who can get a prototype out of an AI chat and want to share it as a real link.
